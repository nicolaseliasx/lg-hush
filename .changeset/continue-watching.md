Home is now only Continue Watching, Favorites and Inputs. The "For You" row shows the titles in progress in your Stremio library (episode, time left and a progress bar; OK opens the title in Stremio Cards); sign in once with `scripts/stremio-login.sh`, which saves only the session key on the TV. The public-catalogue recommendations are gone. Removed: the greeting, date and weather widgets and the webhook shortcuts (their saved settings are ignored), so Settings → Widgets lists only Continue Watching and Inputs. Favorites sit on a dock in a slightly lighter black, like Apple TV's, and their cards are 2% larger (the Apps grid is unchanged). The Luma logo is gone: the app icon is a plain "H", and the QR codes and the wallpaper upload page carry no logo.
